# Parchmatte Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 25, 2026_

Parchmatte is made by Galactic Luddite.

## What we collect

Nothing. Parchmatte does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any
personal data. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or
third-party code, and it makes no network connections.

## What stays on your Mac

Your preferences (texture, strength, lamp, schedule, excluded apps and
similar) are saved in the app's local preferences file on your Mac so they
persist between launches. They never leave your device.

To position window covers, Parchmatte asks macOS for the position and size of
on-screen windows. The only window information it reads is each window's
bounds, window number, owning process ID, owning application name, layer and
whether it is on screen. The application name is used only to recognise
system overviews such as Mission Control. It does not read window contents
or titles, and does not record or store this information.

Sunrise and sunset times are estimated from your Mac's time-zone setting.
Parchmatte does not request or use your location.

## Deleting your data

Deleting Parchmatte removes its preferences. You can also reset them with:

```sh
defaults delete com.galacticluddite.parchmatte
```

## Third parties

Parchmatte shares no data with anyone, because it has none to share.

## Contact

Questions: support@parchmatte.com

The app also recognizes macOS’s interactive screenshot picker from public
window owner, layer, and bounds metadata. It temporarily removes its own
panels from window selection. It does not inspect screenshots, window titles,
or the keys used to open the picker.
